TERMINA 80

Product file · first machine

Termina 80

A retro micro-computer on an ESP32 with a 3.5 inch screen. It has no keyboard of its own: the keyboard and mouse of your PC reach it over Wi-Fi. It boots to a shell, runs Lua programs, and ships with a windowed desktop.

Decree n° 84-001 on home computing

What every Vendetta machine owes you

Article 1

It belongs to you.No account to create and no cloud to sign in to. You plug it in and it boots to a prompt.

Article 2

You can make it yourself.The case prints in 3D, the firmware flashes from a PC, and every part is easy to find.

Article 3

The screen glows.Green, amber or white phosphor, with scanlines if you want them. Nobody needs a flat grey terminal.
