Company

An independent studio

Vendetta 84 builds low-tech, alternative computing: modest machines that do a few things well, run on little power and stay under your control. An answer to devices that are disposable and impossible to repair. Every design is open source, and the machines grow with a community of users who build, fix and share software for them.
